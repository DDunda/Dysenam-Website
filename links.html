Links

YouTube

  • MikeMakesMoreMusic — A cool channel centered on videogame music deconstructions, particularly UNDERTALE and DELTARUNE.
  • Orj — Another deconstruction channel with a broader selection of videogames, and hidden gems.

Games

  • Every 5x5 Nonogram — A fun experiment in solving every 5x5 nonogram grid by hand (all 25 million).

Tools

  • OKLCH.com — A nice little colour picker based on OkLCH, with 3–4 dynamically shifting picker windows with different constrained axes.
  • MSDF SVG Converter — Despite the limited interface this is a fast and effective tool for converting svgs to MSDFs (bitmap SDFs with sharp corners).
  • DYLMWYTDW — A silly website based on making music with meme soundbytes. People have made some quality stuff with it.
  • ColorEasily — A nice utility for creating website colour schemes, particularly for its in-built assessments of contrast.
